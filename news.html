BLVD 1.3.1 GitHub →

New

  • A lean package: from 270 files down to 10. .NET, WPF and the libraries go inside BLVD.exe; beside it are only BLVD's 5 native DLLs, the licences, the readme and the guide.
  • Those who update from an earlier version with Update now have the old leftover files deleted automatically on the first start (settings and your other files stay).

Notes

  • On the first start, Windows extracts parts of .NET to a temporary folder; it may take a second or two longer than the next ones.
  • Problems and suggestions: open an issue in this repository.

BLVD 1.3.0 GitHub →

New

Material

  • 3D preview of the VMT as you edit it: sphere, cube or plane, with texture, colour, transparency, bump-map relief and the shine of $envmap/$phong. Finds the textures in the VMT's materials folder and in Garry's Mod's content.

Textures

  • Generate normal maps from the colours: in Convert (for every image without a _normal, already linked as $bumpmap) and in the Viewer (Edit → Generate normal map…).
  • Convert history: the last 10 conversions, to repeat with one click.

Props

  • Props page: every compiled prop, with a picture, the path for Hammer/Lua, size, triangles and LODs; copy the path, open the folder, delete.
  • Skins ($texturegroup): the same model with other textures, with a selector in the preview.
  • Physics props (prop_physics): mass estimated from the volume and the surface property, and prop_data chosen by size.
  • Signs from text: type, pick a font, colour and outline, and go on to the same window as From image.
  • Glowing signs: the front lights up in the dark ($selfillum), whole or only its bright parts.

Maps

  • Pack into the map…: puts the custom content inside a copy of the .bsp (name_packed.bsp); the original stays as it is.

Help

  • A Help button on the left bar: opens the guide at the section of the page you are on, in the app's language.

Notes

  • Packing content changes the map's checksum: players with the old version need the new one.
  • The Explorer menus (Open with BLVD, Convert to VTF) stay in English.
  • Problems and suggestions: open an issue in this repository.

New

  • Brazilian Portuguese. BLVD still opens in English; in Settings → Language pick Português (Brasil) and click Restart now.

  • The Map page is now only the map content check: Overview and signs was removed.

Fixes

  • BLVD closed with an error (and didn't save its settings) when the window was closed maximized or minimized.

Notes

  • The Explorer menus (Open with BLVD, Convert to VTF) stay in English.
  • On Windows 11 they are under Show more options.
  • Problems and suggestions: open an issue in this repository.

BLVD 1.1.0 GitHub →

New

In-app updates

  • Update now: when a new version is out, BLVD downloads the right package, checks its SHA256, replaces the files and restarts, keeping your settings. Works from this version on (users of 1.0.0 download this one here).

Model

  • From image…: turns an image (logo, sign, plate) into a solid prop, with holes, darker sides and one part per piece. Live outline, exact numbers for every option and size in units (height, width and thickness).
  • Collision fitted to the shape (new default): convex pieces that keep gaps, the inside of letters and the space under tables open, with a piece limit (Most pieces, default 16).
  • Compiled props face +X, as in Hammer.
  • A notice when the model seems to lie down, with Stand it up.
  • 3D preview on a dark, light or checkerboard background with a ground grid; Compiled shows the prop read back from the files written, with each LOD.

Convert

  • Linked maps: _normal and _spec become the same VMT's $bumpmap and $envmapmask.
  • Numbered images become one animated VTF (optional).
  • Preview of what will be written, without writing anything.
  • Files dropped anywhere on the window go to the right page.

Images

  • WebP (and AVIF/HEIC with the Microsoft Store extensions) and SVG/SVGZ everywhere in the app. SVGs keep their proportions when they become textures.

Map (new page)

  • Checks the content of a .bsp or .vmf: what is missing, what is custom (must ship with the map) and what comes from the Workshop or mounted games. Copy custom content… gathers the custom files into an addon.
  • Overview and signs: turns a top-down render into a street map with traffic signs and road markings, and exports the image or a Source overview. (Removed in 1.2.0.)

Fixes

  • Convert closed on its own when converting more than about five images.
  • The collision's convex hull could take minutes on thin or very small parts.
  • "Could not load" when opening a WebP image in From image….
  • Small filled holes in logos with transparency showed black.

Notes

  • The Explorer menus keep the executable's path: if you move the folder, turn them off and on again in Settings.
  • On Windows 11, Open with BLVD and Convert to VTF are under Show more options.
  • Problems and suggestions: open an issue in this repository.

BLVD 1.0.0 GitHub →

What BLVD does

  • Viewer: zoom at the cursor, RGBA channels, transparency, tiling, frames/faces/slices/mipmaps, animation, HDR; editing of version, flags, reflectivity and resources; Save/Export/Copy/Paste with alpha; tabs (Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W, Ctrl+Tab).
  • Edit: rotate, flip, resize, invert the green channel (normal maps), swap R/B and regenerate mipmaps.
  • Material: a VMT editor with syntax colours and validation; a Create… form with a preview.
  • Convert: selected images or whole folders, with progress, cancelling, a log and Watch (converts again on save).
  • Model: turns a model (OBJ, FBX, glTF, DAE, 3DS…) into a Garry's Mod static prop without Blender: converted textures and VMTs, collision (a convex hull of the whole model, one per part, or the file's collision mesh), hitboxes, automatic LODs, a texture size limit and only the files Garry's Mod uses. A 3D preview with collision, hitboxes and an info_player_start at real scale; units, axis, rotation and size; the final size estimated before compiling. Uses the studiomdl of the installed Garry's Mod.
  • Windows integration (Settings, per user, no administrator): Open with BLVD on .vtf/.vmt, Convert to VTF on images (a window with the VTF and VMT folders, the $basetexture path and the whole VMT) and .vtf thumbnails in Explorer.

Notes

  • Those who used the earlier test versions (3.0.0-preview) don't get this update notice: download it here. Settings don't carry over to the new version on their own.
  • The Explorer menus keep the executable's path: if you move the folder, turn them off and on again in Settings.
  • On Windows 11, Open with BLVD and Convert to VTF are under Show more options.
  • Problems and suggestions: open an issue in this repository.
